# Ideology / Religion

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Purpose

Provide a cultural or ideological influence layer for factions where it creates meaningful strategic consequences.

## Current Direction

The system is intended to affect diplomacy, stability, territory, faction mechanics, and events. Different factions do not necessarily interact with it symmetrically.

## Open Questions

- universal versus faction-specific system;
- spread model;
- territorial effects;
- conversion/resistance;
- relationship to politics, diplomacy, and rebellion.
