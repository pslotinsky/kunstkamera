# Strategic Resources

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Purpose

Make particular locations valuable for reasons other than generic income and create dependencies that affect expansion, trade, technology, and warfare.

## Decisions

- Money remains the universal basic economic currency.
- Strategic resources are additional scarce resources, not replacements for money.

## Open Questions

- number and categories of resources;
- stockpiled versus access-based model;
- extraction requirements;
- consumption model;
- which ships/buildings/technologies require them;
- trade and blockade interactions.
