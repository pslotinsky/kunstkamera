# Diplomacy

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Purpose

Allow major factions to manage conflict and cooperation without reducing all interaction to immediate warfare.

## Planned Scope

- diplomatic relations;
- war and peace;
- non-aggression;
- trade agreements;
- military access;
- alliances;
- territorial demands;
- vassalage;
- reputation/trust or equivalent consequences for commitments.

## Prototype

- Every faction, including neutral factions, is hostile to every other faction.
- There are no diplomatic actions.

## Boundaries

Diplomacy supports the territorial strategy game; it is not intended to replace warfare as the primary conflict layer.

## Open Questions

- relationship model;
- negotiation model;
- enforceable versus breakable agreements;
- war goals / peace settlement model;
- exact vassal rules;
- interaction with faction identity, politics, espionage, and AI.
