# Characters

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Purpose

Represent important individuals whose development and traits affect strategic decisions.

## Planned Roles

- rulers;
- Heroes / fleet commanders;
- governors;
- faction-specific special characters.

Not every faction must use every role in the same way.

## Heroes and Fleets

Current direction for the full game:

- Heroes can be recruited on eligible owned planets.
- A Hero commands a mobile fleet/army.
- Squad recruitment is performed by a Hero while that Hero's fleet is located in an eligible owned star system.
- Recruitment orders belong to the Hero's fleet rather than to the planet or system.
- A Hero whose fleet has active recruitment orders cannot move until those orders complete or are cancelled.
- Planetary Garrison remains independent of Heroes and mobile fleets.

The prototype does not require Heroes yet. When recruitment is started from a planet, it creates an unnamed empty fleet as a temporary stand-in for the future Hero-led army.

## Current Direction

Characters are intended to affect military command, movement, logistics, planetary administration, diplomacy, and faction-specific systems. Character attributes should remain limited to values that produce actual strategic differences.

## Open Questions

- Hero recruitment cost and availability;
- whether every full-game fleet must have a Hero;
- progression and skills;
- death/succession;
- loyalty and relationships;
- capture;
- how strongly Heroes modify fleet capacity and combat outcomes.
