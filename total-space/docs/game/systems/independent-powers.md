# Independent Powers

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Purpose

Make the galaxy contain strategically relevant actors beyond the major playable factions.

## Planned Types

- minor independent factions;
- pirates;
- rebels;
- mercenaries;
- nomadic groups.

These categories do not need identical rules.

## Open Questions

- which are persistent political actors versus event-generated forces;
- territorial ownership;
- diplomacy;
- recruitment/mercenary contracts;
- pirate bases and suppression;
- rebellion relationship to former owner.
