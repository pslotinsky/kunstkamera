# Technology

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Purpose

Provide long-term progression and expand strategic capabilities over a campaign.

## Current Direction

Technology can affect ships, buildings, economy, movement, warfare, information, and faction-specific capabilities.

Technology should create new options and strategic differentiation rather than exist only as a stack of small percentage bonuses.

## Dependencies

- factions;
- ships and recruitment;
- buildings and economy;
- strategic resources;
- campaign pacing.

## Open Questions

- research resource and generation;
- tree, branches, tiers, or another structure;
- shared versus faction-specific technologies;
- unlocks versus upgrades;
- mutually exclusive choices;
- technology trading/espionage interactions.
