# Planets

**Status:** DRAFT

Owns what a Planet is, its place in a star system, and its ownership. Development through the Settlement is defined in [buildings.md](buildings.md).

## Rules

- Planet is the primary territorial and development location.
- Every planet belongs to one star system and has its own position in galaxy space.
- Each system has one main planet and zero or more secondary planets.
- Every planet has an owner in the prototype: either a playable faction or a neutral faction.
- Planets can be captured independently from other planets in the same system.
- Every authored planet has its own identity and definition; planets of the same broad kind can still differ materially.
- A planet's development level is the level of its Settlement building; see [buildings.md](buildings.md).
- A planet cannot contain every type of infrastructure.
- Every planet has one Garrison; see [fleets.md](fleets.md) and [planetary-assault.md](planetary-assault.md).

## Prototype

- No population simulation beyond the abstract population represented by Settlement development.
- No habitability model.
- No planet-kind gameplay modifiers.
- No occupation or integration phase.
