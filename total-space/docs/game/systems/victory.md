# Victory & Campaign

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Prototype Rule

- A faction is eliminated when it owns no planets.
- The prototype has no grace period for recovering a lost final planet.
- The last remaining faction wins.

## Full-Game Purpose

Define how campaigns begin, progress, end, and communicate success or defeat.

## Planned Scope

Candidate full-game victory paths: territorial, military, economic, technological, diplomatic, and faction-specific.

## Open Questions

- Default full-game victory condition.
- Whether all victory paths are available to all factions.
- Faction-specific victory objectives.
- Campaign length targets.
- Turn limits.
- Short/scenario/custom campaign modes.
- Anti-snowball mechanisms.
- Progress visibility.
