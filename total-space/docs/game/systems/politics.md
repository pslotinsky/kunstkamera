# Internal Politics

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Purpose

Create internal constraints and pressures so expansion and faction management are not determined only by external enemies.

## Planned Elements

Potential committed areas from the current design include:

- stability/order;
- political groups or interests;
- influence or equivalent political resource;
- political missions/demands;
- government-specific structures such as councils or senates;
- succession or political change where relevant to a faction.

## Open Questions

- universal system versus faction-specific politics;
- how much politics directly constrains player actions;
- relationship to rulers/characters;
- relationship to rebellion and conquered territory;
- whether influence is a universal resource.
