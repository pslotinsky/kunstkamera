# Espionage

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Purpose

Provide information warfare and limited indirect action between factions.

## Planned Scope

- intelligence gathering;
- reconnaissance beyond ordinary visibility;
- counter-intelligence;
- sabotage or other covert actions;
- risk of detection and diplomatic consequences.

## Boundaries

Espionage should support strategic decisions on the main campaign map rather than become a separate tactical minigame.

## Open Questions

- agents versus abstract network;
- action/resource model;
- available information targets;
- sabotage scope;
- discovery and attribution;
- relationship to characters and diplomacy.
