# Total Space — Vision

**Status:** DRAFT

## High Concept

Turn-based space grand strategy focused on territorial warfare, empire development, and fleet positioning.

The campaign structure is inspired primarily by the strategic layer of **Total War**. Space warfare is resolved strategically rather than through direct control of individual ships.

## Core Loop

1. Expand into new systems and planets.
2. Develop planets and infrastructure.
3. Recruit ship squads and form fleets.
4. Position fleets across the galaxy.
5. Fight enemy armies.
6. Assault and capture planets.
7. Consolidate territory and continue expansion.
