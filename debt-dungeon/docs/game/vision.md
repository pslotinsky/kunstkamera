# Debt Dungeon — Vision

**Status:** CONCEPT

## High Concept

A strategy game about managing a magical labyrinth. A person buried in debt accidentally becomes the owner of a long-abandoned labyrinth. To survive, he repels an initial attack, then discovers that crystals obtained in the dungeon can be sold for substantial sums in the ordinary world.

The protagonist's initial goal is to repay his debts. Gradually, the labyrinth becomes his own domain, and its inhabitants become allies for whom he feels responsible.

## Core Loop

1. Explore the dungeon and learn about neighboring labyrinths.
2. Gather resources and provide for the inhabitants.
3. Build and develop the labyrinth, recruiting workers and troops.
4. Defend, wage war, negotiate, and expand territory, transforming the land around the labyrinth.
5. Develop creatures, unlock new opportunities, and venture deeper into the dungeon.
6. Decide whether to spend crystals on the labyrinth or exchange them for money in the ordinary world.

## Core Experience

- A domain that grows from an abandoned labyrinth into a thriving state.
- Distinct labyrinths with their own cultures, creatures, buildings, and strategies.
- Personal attachment to creatures through experience, evolution, and unique heroes.
- Politics and conflict between labyrinths; not every conflict has to end in destruction.
- The contrast between everyday human debts and a fantastical underground world.

## Military Model

Positional turn-based battles inspired by Disciples, with a commander-led squad as the combat unit. Each squad contains troops of one type; its commander may be an ordinary character or a unique hero. Details belong to the [troops system](system/troops.md).

## Main References

**Age of Wonders, Disciples, Total War, and Thea.** Confirmed areas of influence are described in [references](references.md).

## Open Questions

Detailed positional combat rules, troop scale, the labyrinth management interface, the precise structure of strategic turns, and territory expansion rules. The overall combat direction and the principle of terrain transformation have already been chosen.
