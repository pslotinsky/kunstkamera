# Ordinary World

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Purpose

The ordinary human world is a second layer of the game. It connects fantasy development to the protagonist's original everyday goal: getting out of debt.

Crystals from the dungeon can be sold for money in the ordinary world. The proceeds allow the protagonist to repay debts and improve his living conditions. After repaying his debts, he may continue furnishing his home and buying expensive possessions—possibly as a small optional minigame.

## Influence on the Dungeon

The concept includes the possibility of buying human weapons and other goods for use in the labyrinth. Quests from the ordinary world that are completed in the dungeon are also possible.

Human expeditions seeking rare materials may provide another source of interaction and conflict; the events themselves are described in [events](events.md).

## Priority

This layer should reinforce motivation and the contrast between worlds, without quietly turning into a second standalone simulation.

## Open Questions

How often the protagonist returns, how interactive his home is, and which human goods meaningfully change underground strategy.
