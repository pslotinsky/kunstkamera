# Dungeon

**Status:** CONCEPT

## World

The dungeon consists of several floors. Each floor contains multiple independent labyrinths, which may trade, maintain friendly or neutral relations, or wage war.

On deeper floors, labyrinths tend to be wealthier and stronger. This provides a long-term direction for exploration and development.

Labyrinth territories within floors can expand and transform the terrain; rules for this layer are described in [territory](territory.md).

## Inter-Floor Restriction

Creatures lose some of their strength when moving **to either the next or the previous floor**. Large-scale wars between floors are therefore relatively rare, while conflicts between labyrinths on the same floor are much more frequent.

This restriction creates strategic separation between floors without making travel and interaction impossible.

## Responsibility

This document defines the structure of the entire dungeon and relationships between floors. Management of individual domains is described in [labyrinths](labyrinths.md), and political relationships in [diplomacy](diplomacy.md).

## Open Questions

The number of floors and labyrinths, the layout of connections, ways of unlocking new floors, and the nature of inter-floor weakening and whether it can be overcome.
