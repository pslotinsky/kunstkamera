# Economy

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Basic Needs

The labyrinth needs energy and food to sustain itself and develop. Magical crystals are the key resource: they provide energy and are spent on construction, recruitment, upgrades, and creature upkeep.

Crystals may remain after creatures die. Hunting and specialized production facilities or farms may provide additional ways to obtain resources.

Creatures also need food, which must be obtained. Food shortages can be compensated for by spending crystals. The exact substitution mechanism remains undefined.

## Central Dilemma

Crystals can be exchanged for money in the ordinary world. The player chooses between developing and protecting the labyrinth today and repaying the protagonist's personal debts.

This should connect both parts of the game without necessarily introducing a complex financial simulation.

## Boundaries

Buildings and research belong to [development](development.md); spending money in the human world belongs to the [ordinary world](real-world.md).

## Open Questions

The precise relationship between crystals and energy, food production, additional resources, balancing constraints, and the exchange rate in the ordinary world.
