# Labyrinth Development

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Buildings

The domain develops through restoring, constructing, and upgrading buildings. Buildings create new opportunities for economic activity, recruitment, defense, creature evolution, and research.

Different labyrinths may have unique buildings and their own development paths. These differences should affect play style rather than be purely cosmetic.

## Labyrinth Level

A labyrinth's level reflects the domain's overall development and unlocks new opportunities. The precise level requirements and limitations remain unknown.

## Research

Research may take place in dedicated buildings and require suitable workers and resources. It unlocks new opportunities, specializations, and improvements.

## Connections

Development costs are defined by the [economy](economy.md); species progression by [creatures](creatures.md); each domain's individual identity by [labyrinths](labyrinths.md).

## Open Questions

Building slots versus free placement, construction time, the technology tree, whether research is required, and its dependence on races.
