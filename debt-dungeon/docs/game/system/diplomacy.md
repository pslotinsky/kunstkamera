# Diplomacy and Relationships

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Relationships Between Labyrinths

Neighboring labyrinths may be hostile, neutral, or friendly. Interaction extends beyond warfare: alliances, voluntary agreements, subjugation, and vassalage are possible.

A defeated labyrinth can be destroyed, subjugated, or made a vassal. Establishing friendly relations is an independent alternative to conquest.

## Racial Relationships

Different races and creature species have different attitudes toward one another. The proposed basis is a two-axis alignment model: **good ↔ evil** and **order ↔ chaos**, including neutral positions. This is a **candidate model**, not a finalized system.

Recruiting creatures with opposing, incompatible alignments should create difficulties, while compatible groups should offer additional opportunities. The precise consequences remain to be defined.

## Boundaries

This document describes political relationships and compatibility; specific races and labyrinths are listed under [content](../content/README.md).

## Open Questions

How relationships change, who can enter agreements, how much autonomy vassals retain, and how incompatible creatures coexist.
