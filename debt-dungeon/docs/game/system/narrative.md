# Narrative

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Premise

The protagonist has hit rock bottom and can no longer pay his bills. While fleeing debt collectors—or wandering in a hunger-induced delirium—he accidentally enters a dungeon. Inside an abandoned labyrinth, he discovers a throne room. An unexpected congratulatory announcement declares him the new owner: the labyrinth has long awaited a master and can supposedly flourish again.

The introduction to the world is intended primarily for the protagonist himself: he does not understand what he has acquired or how to manage it. An attack begins almost immediately. He is forced to take charge to stay alive.

## Evolving Motivation

The first successful battle leaves behind magical crystals that can be sold for a substantial sum. The protagonist sees a way to repay his debts. Over time, he becomes involved in conflicts between labyrinths, grows attached to his domain and its inhabitants, makes friends, and acquires enemies.

## Possible Long-Term Conflict

Deep within the dungeon, a powerful labyrinth may seek to subjugate the others and ultimately destroy humanity. This is a **story possibility**, not a mandatory ending.

## Tone

The contrast between an absurd everyday debt situation and serious fantasy politics allows humor, adventure, and personal stakes to coexist.

## Open Questions

The labyrinth's origins, the identity of whoever announces the new owner, the reason for the protagonist's arrival, and the main story's degree of linearity.
