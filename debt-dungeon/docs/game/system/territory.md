# Territory and Terrain Transformation

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Labyrinth Territory

Each labyrinth has territory that it can expand. Expansion transforms the surrounding land into that labyrinth's terrain type.

Different labyrinths can share the same terrain type. Terrain type is not a unique identifier for a domain or race.

## Terrain Types

Preliminary options include swamp, desert, grassland, caves, cemetery, snow, and lava. This list is open and does not define a final set of types or their distribution among labyrinths.

## Transformation

As its territory expands, a labyrinth spreads its terrain type onto new land. The principle of transformation is confirmed; its speed, conditions, and specific gameplay consequences remain undefined.

The references for this direction are **Disciples** and **Age of Wonders**. They provide inspiration for territorial expansion and land transformation, rather than a requirement to copy their specific rules.

## Connections

## Open Questions

Methods of territorial expansion and conquest, how borders are represented on the map, transformation speed, competing influences from neighbors, the state of land after control is lost, and terrain effects on creatures, resources, and battles.
