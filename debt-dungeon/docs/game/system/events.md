# Events, Quests, and Expeditions

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Purpose

Events make the dungeon a living world, create unexpected opportunities, and connect the economy, warfare, characters, and narrative.

Possible sources of events:

- attacks by neighboring labyrinths;
- discoveries during scouting;
- quests from the human world;
- human expeditions seeking unique materials;
- acquiring unique heroes;
- conflicts between races and labyrinths;
- threats that emerge as the player ventures deeper.

Some events may be story-driven; others may be systemic or random. Events should not constantly interrupt labyrinth management without meaningful consequences.

## Boundaries

The mandatory opening attack belongs to the [narrative](narrative.md). This document describes the system for subsequent events and interactions.

## Open Questions

Quest format, event frequency, player choices, long-term consequences, and the effects of the protagonist's actions on the underground world.
