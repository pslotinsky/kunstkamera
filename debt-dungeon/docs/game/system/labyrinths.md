# Labyrinths

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Definition

A labyrinth is an independent domain with its own owner, inhabitants, economy, military forces, and characteristics. The player's starting labyrinth has long been abandoned and is in decline; the first stage aims to bring it back to life.

Each labyrinth has its own identity: its resident races, characteristic buildings, resources, and development features. Labyrinths may pursue different strategies and adopt different political positions.

A labyrinth has a development level. Raising its level expands the domain's capabilities; specific effects are defined in [development](development.md).

## Territory

A labyrinth has an expandable territory and an associated terrain type. Land transformation and the possibility of different domains sharing a terrain type are described in [territory](territory.md).

## Strategic Role

Labyrinths are the main units of ownership and rivalry. They can be attacked, defended, and brought under political influence. Destruction, subjugation, alliances, and vassalage belong to [diplomacy](diplomacy.md).

## Open Questions

How a labyrinth's territory and center are represented on the map, how a domain is defeated, and what opportunities become available after acquiring another labyrinth.
