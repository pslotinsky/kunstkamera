# Workers

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Role

Worker creatures sustain the labyrinth and enable its growth. Unlike troops, their main value lies in performing economic and support tasks.

Preliminary areas of work:

- scouting and discovering new opportunities;
- construction and restoration;
- gathering crystals and other resources;
- hunting and food production;
- research in appropriate buildings;
- other specialized functions associated with particular races.

Worker capabilities may vary between labyrinths and creature species. Their experience and personal progression belong to [creatures](creatures.md), while resource consumption and production belong to the [economy](economy.md).

## Open Questions

Whether workers need manual assignment, automatic employment, or a combination of both; whether individual creatures can switch between economic and combat roles.
