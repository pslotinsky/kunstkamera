# Troops

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Military Role

Troops defend the labyrinth, fight wars against neighbors, and allow it to extend its influence within the dungeon.

Preliminary creature combat roles:

- melee;
- ranged;
- magic;
- artillery.

The available troop roster depends on the labyrinth and its resident races. Creatures can gain experience and evolve; these rules belong to [creatures](creatures.md).

## Command

The combat unit is a squad consisting of a commander and troops of a single type. Mixed troop types within one squad are not allowed. The commander may be an ordinary character or a unique hero.

A squad acts as a single combat unit. The commander and the troops under their command are separate components of the squad; requirements for matching the commander's species to the troop type remain undefined.

## Battles

The chosen direction is positional turn-based combat close to **Disciples**. A commander-led squad occupies a combat position in place of an individual creature. The player controls squads as whole combat units rather than moving individual fighters.

The specific position layout, action order, and targeting rules still need to be designed.

## Open Questions

The number of squads in an army, the number of troops in a squad, combat position layout, action order, range and targeting, the effects of troop numbers and casualties on effectiveness, commander abilities, their participation in attacks, and their fate when a squad is defeated.
