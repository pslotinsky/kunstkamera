# Creatures: Individuality and Development

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Creatures

Labyrinths are inhabited by different kinds of creatures. Within a single race, creatures may have different specializations, combat roles, and worker roles. Creatures need not be reduced to interchangeable resource units: individuals may have their own experience and history.

## Progression

Creatures gain experience, improve, and evolve. Evolution paths and available forms may depend on their species and the labyrinth's development. Connections to buildings are described in [development](development.md).

## Rarity and Heroes

Creatures may differ in their degree of uniqueness. Unique heroes play a special role: these rare characters may join after the defeat of another labyrinth or through special events.

A squad commander may be either an ordinary character or a unique hero. Squad composition and combat actions are described in [troops](troops.md); the specific effects of commander abilities remain undefined.

## Intent

Players should be able to become attached to individual inhabitants rather than perceive them solely as army numbers.

## Open Questions

The extent of individual statistics, possible death or capture, branching evolutions, experience inheritance, and hero recruitment mechanics. It also remains open how experience and evolution are tracked for rank-and-file troops within a single-type squad and separately for its commander.
