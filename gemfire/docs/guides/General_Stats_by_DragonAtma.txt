   This is a list of the 64 generals that appear in Gemfire (aka Royal Blood 
in Japan). The first section contains a list of the generals' stats, while the
second section contains a list of who they serve (if any) in which scenario.
Thanks to Atma playing lots of RoTK games (a turn-based version of Dynasty
Warriors), I am assuming that scenario 1 is 201 AD (which makes scenarios 2-4
206 AD, 211 AD, and 216 AD). Also note that only 28 of the 64 generals are
actually attached to a family -- that means that if one of the others rebels,
their last name can (and often will) vary from game to game. Note that I've
sorted the generals by their force (or primary force if they serve more than
one): Blanche, Lyle, Chrysalis, Molbrew, Flax, Coryll, Tate, Tordin, Divas,
Tudoria, and finally Lankshire. Finally, four of the generals are female --
this is signified by the -F added to their name.

   As of version 1.03, minor widespread updates have been added because of a
certain... issue. You see, most officers receive either +1 to all three stats
each January or no boost at all, whereas force leaders receive either +2 to
all stats or no boost at all. Therefore, scenario 1 Erin will be either
82/87/76 or 84/89/78 after the first chance to be boosted, whereas scenario 1
Karl is either 76/57/77 or 77/58/78. But that applies to EVERY January, so
they may get the boost before you even choose a force! Therefore, about a
third of the officers were either a point or two higher than the base stats
listed here. D'OH!

And no, the odds aren't the same for everyone. Scenario 4 Seth, for example,
almost never gets that boost in the first year.
