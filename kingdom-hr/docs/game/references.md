# Kingdom HR — References

**Status:** CONCEPT

These references identify specific ideas worth studying, not games that Kingdom HR is meant to reproduce in their entirety. They refer to existing games rather than our own design proposals.

## Papers, Please

**Screenshot:** [Gameplay screenshot](https://www.pcgames.de/screenshots/1280x/2013/08/Papers_06-pc-games.jpg)

**What to study:** The feeling of working at a desk, closely examining documents, comparing details, and making small decisions with serious consequences.

**Not automatically adopting:** Passport inspection or a mandatory daily routine of repetitive checks against rigid rules.

## This Is the Police

**Screenshot:** [Gameplay screenshot](https://www.gamepressure.com/thisisthepolice/gfx/word/515278182.jpg)

**What to study:** Staff management, day-to-day work, personal stories, fatigue, personnel problems, and the relationship between routine work and narrative.

**Difference:** In Kingdom HR, the appointed leaders make operational decisions themselves.

## Majesty: The Fantasy Kingdom Sim

**Screenshot:** [Gameplay screenshot](https://www.old-games.ru/games/pc/majesty_the_fantasy_kingdom_sim___northern_expansion/screenshots/811_66046e05ed85b.jpg)

**What to study:** Indirect control and the independent behavior of hired characters.

**Difference:** Kingdom HR focuses on personnel decisions rather than construction and assigning tasks to heroes.

## Crusader Kings III

**Screenshot:** [Gameplay screenshot](https://steamcdn-a.akamaihd.net/steam/apps/1158310/ss_6a3025e5e669b26067a381b18a71cf426dd76a61.1920x1080.jpg)

**What to study:** Personality traits, loyalty, personal relationships, political ambitions, and conflicts among people in power.

**Difference:** Kingdom HR does not aim to provide the full set of grand-strategy controls.

## King of Dragon Pass / Six Ages

**Screenshot:** [Six Ages event screenshot](https://clan.fastly.steamstatic.com/images/32978489/a3022d0f89ec07b0cc3e2dcbfec9e5a750fb09a5.jpg)

**What to study:** Branching events, context-dependent consequences, character advisors, and stories emerging from the interaction of events and the state of the realm.

**Difference:** Choosing whom to hire is Kingdom HR's central action, rather than one aspect of governing a clan.

## Reigns

**Screenshot:** [Gameplay screenshot](https://www.reignsgames.com/assets/reigns-walkthrough-swipe-king/reigns-famine-refusal-supplies-protect-money-villager-loss.webp)

**What to study:** Compact events, consequences of decisions, and replayability.

**Difference:** Events in Kingdom HR should largely grow out of the specific people hired and their personal stories.

## The Shrouded Isle

**Screenshot:** [Gameplay screenshot](https://static.fanbyte.com/uploads/rs/assets/editorial/2017/04/shroudedisle5.jpg)

**What to study:** Appointing people with incomplete information, hidden traits, and the need to understand characters over time.

## Kingdom / Life Choices

**Screenshot:** [Life Choices choice screen](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/785231462327997468/66805CB7A0B0E30DFF7C1B8546C05D44C9417B94/)

**What to study:** Choices within events and the different consequences they can produce, as raised during our discussion.

**Clarification:** The exact titles or entries have not yet been established. In particular, "Kingdom" should not automatically be interpreted as Kingdom: Two Crowns.

## Romance of the Three Kingdoms XI

**Screenshot:** [City administration interface](https://media.moddb.com/images/games/1/35/34174/32212_City-Info-B2_medium.jpg)

**What to study:** Free officers are associated with specific cities. The player can search controlled cities for officers and try to recruit them; taking new cities expands the places where searches can be conducted.

**Source:** [Personnel commands in the game guide](https://www.neoseeker.com/rottk-xi/faqs/153966-walkthrough.html).

## Nobunaga's Ambition: Iron Triangle

**Screenshot:** [Recruiting a rōnin at Inabayama Castle](https://cdn.mobygames.com/ba397f74-c27f-11ed-bae4-02420a0001a1.webp)

**What to study:** Unaffiliated officers (rōnin) can be found and recruited within controlled territory. Recruiting talent is therefore linked to territorial reach, alongside the possibility of attracting officers from rival clans.

**Source:** [Game manual — Personnel commands](https://www.videogamemanual.com/PS2/Nobunaga%27s%20Ambition-%20Iron%20Triangle%20%28USA%29.pdf).
