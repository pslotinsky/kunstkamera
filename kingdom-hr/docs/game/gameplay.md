# Kingdom HR — Gameplay

**Status:** CONCEPT

## Daily Loop

Each turn represents one day. During the day, the player can:

1. Review the kingdom's current state, occupied positions, and vacancies that urgently need filling.
2. Create and publish vacancies, specifying a specialty and minimum competencies in it.
3. Review applications: discard a résumé, set it aside, or invite the candidate to an interview.
4. Conduct interviews and meetings with current employees. Both use the same limited daily allowance.
5. Hire an interviewed candidate immediately or ask them to wait. Previously interviewed candidates can be hired at any time while they remain available.

The player ends the turn, advancing life in all kingdoms automatically.

At the start of the next day, the player receives:

- News about changes in the kingdom: provinces gained or lost, rebellions, epidemics, and other events.
- Work reports about changes in staff competencies and morale, plus individual reports when relevant: level gains, intentions to resign, fights, or discoveries of ancient artifacts.
- New applications for published vacancies. Applications arrive gradually; some days may bring none.

The player reviews the new situation and repeats the loop.

## Waiting Candidates

The player sees how long each candidate has waited, but does not know how much longer they will wait. Some may wait indefinitely; others may leave after a few days, accept another kingdom's offer, or pursue something else.

Prolonged lack of a response almost guarantees a worsening attitude toward the state. Some candidates may hold a grudge with later consequences. Delaying a decision after an interview has more severe consequences than delaying résumé review.

## Assessing Employees

Individual contributions are not shown directly. The player assesses employees through news and reports, compares economic, scientific, healthcare, and other charts with hiring dates, and considers personal events, mood, attitude toward the state, and opinions from subordinates and superiors.

## Campaign Goal

The goal is the kingdom's victory over the others. The initial version can focus on military victory. Economic, cultural, and other paths, including provinces voluntarily asking to join, may be considered later.

## Initial Scope

The daily loop above is the proposed MVP scope. Vacations, salaries, career progression, and training courses can be considered later.
