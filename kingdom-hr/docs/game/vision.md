# Kingdom HR — Vision

**Status:** CONCEPT

Kingdom HR is a strategy game in which the player does not govern a kingdom directly, but manages the people who do. Instead of personally developing cities, researching technology, waging wars, and conducting diplomacy, the player hires the right people for the right positions. Those people act independently, guided by their abilities, personalities, and interests.

The player's main job is to find promising candidates, examine their résumés, conduct interviews, appoint them to positions, and assess their performance. Talented employees can be developed from inexperienced juniors, found on the job market, or poached from rival kingdoms. Strong performers can be promoted, underperformers dismissed, while burnout, corruption, ambition, and conflicts between colleagues all demand attention.

Every person hired may bring a story of their own. Interviews, one-on-one meetings, and personnel decisions shape relationships and trigger events with consequences for both employees and the kingdom. The player does not always know who they are hiring or where a decision will lead.

At the start of a campaign, the player can choose which kingdom to work for. Each has its own challenges, people, and working conditions. As the kingdom develops, its needs and recruitment opportunities change. Its history emerges primarily from decisions about whom the player chooses to trust with power.
