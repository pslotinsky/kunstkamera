# Gemstorm

A fantasy strategy game of rival houses, province management, and tactical battles across Aureth.

Aureth's storm beacons are failing. Its houses disagree over who should restore the wards and hold the seven Gems. Build a secure realm, win the service of their Guardians, and unite the island before the storms divide it permanently.

Gemstorm is heavily influenced by **Koei's Gemfire**. The original game and external guides informed research into its strategic and tactical systems. Gemstorm uses a new setting and cast; historical references are acknowledged in the documentation, not used as the game's branding.
