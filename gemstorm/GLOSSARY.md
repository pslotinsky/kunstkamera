# Gemstorm

Gemstorm is a fantasy strategy game set on Aureth, with its own characters, houses, and fictional history.

## Language

**Aureth**:
The island continent whose thirty provinces form the campaign world.

**House**:
A ruling family that holds provinces, commands generals, and keeps its own treasury of Gems.

**General**:
A member of a house who can govern a province or command its troops.

**Gem Guardian**:
A named being bound to a house's Gem, deployable as an army's Fifth Unit.

**Nerith**:
A tide wyrm that voluntarily joins a province for one battle, separately from hired Fifth Units and Gem Guardians.

**Gemstorm**:
The storm of unnatural power that tore the seven Gems from Varrengard and flung them across Aureth. Each house holds the stones that fell on its land, which sets the houses against one another.
