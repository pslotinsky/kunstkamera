# Asset provenance

The project owner confirmed on 17 November 2026 that the map, portraits, crests, unit art, and music were generated, and that the sound effects are generated or open-source assets. The rebrand retains those files and changes names and references without representing that renaming grants new rights.

The existing [audio design](../design/13-audio.md) records Kenney CC0 packs for selected effects: RPG Audio, Impact Sounds, and Interface Sounds. Other recorded effects and music are described there as authored for the project. Dependencies retain their upstream licences.

The house crests were replaced on 17 November 2026 using the owner-supplied sheet `0a820f6b-ff36-40ae-a252-7a8ad11addb1.png`. Each shield was cropped separately, with detached background specks removed, and exported with transparency as a 188 × 224 WebP at quality 75. The 224-pixel height provides twice the largest nominal in-game display height (112 pixels in the campaign ending). All exports share the same canvas and padding; the shield proportions are preserved. Crest images use smooth browser scaling.

The crests are assigned to fit the houses’ story roles: the royal Varrengard family has the purple shield with three stars, Thornvale has the tree for its forest provinces, and Stormmere keeps the bridge crest. Alderwyn receives the eagle crest. The remaining crests follow the sheet order:

The full third-party guide copies and recognized manual transcription are no longer distributed in the current tree. Their external sources remain linked in [Guides & references](../guides/README.md). Historical research and the name crosswalk may identify Gemfire, its creators, and the source names for attribution. Neither the rebrand nor this provenance record is a legal clearance of generated material, a trademark search, or a rewrite of Git history.
