# Creature name review

This review distinguishes source associations; it is not a legal clearance of a name or artwork.

| Names | Finding | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pastha | The original manual uses this name for its water dragon. No independent traditional source was established here. | Rename to Nerith. |
| Pluvius, Zendor, Empyron, Scylla, Chylla, Skulryk, Dragon | The original game's named Guardian roster. Some names or creature types have older origins; their use as a specific cast is being replaced. | New Guardian names throughout the game; see the crosswalk. |
| Olog-Hai | Appears in Tolkien's *The Lord of the Rings*, Appendix F. It is not a generic name established by the Gemfire research. | Rename to Ironbacks. |
| Orks | A spelling with strong associations to other fantasy properties. No exclusive ownership claim is made here. | Use Grimhides as an editorial choice for the new setting. |
| Airial, Auguskiegies | The original manual uses these spellings; no sufficiently reliable independent source was established in this review. Lack of evidence does not prove uniqueness. | Rename to Stormherald and Granaryfiends. |
| Fachan | Attested in Campbell's nineteenth-century *Popular Tales of the West Highlands*. | Retain the folklore creature type. |
| Far Gorta | Appears in older Irish fairy literature and the Irish folklore archive. | Retain. |
| Gwraig | Related to Welsh folklore; Museum Wales documents Gwraig Annwn. | Retain. |
| Durahan | A transliteration resembling Dullahan, which has older Irish literary sources. | Retain the existing creature label; no claim that this spelling is exclusive. |
| Black Annis | An English folkloric figure rather than a Gemfire invention. | Retain. |
| Bugbear, Gargoyles, Skeletons, Wyverns, Ogre, Lizards; fairies, pixies, redcaps, hobgoblins, leprechauns, unicorns, banshees | Ordinary creature types or folklore labels. | Retain the generic labels and the project's generated artwork. |
| Gunners, Lancers, Shooters, Spearmen, Warriors, Pikemen, Horsemen, Knights, Archers | Descriptive troop and weapon names. | Retain. |

## Sources

- [Original Gemfire manual](https://www.digitpress.com/library/manuals/nes/Gemfire.pdf): the named Guardians and Pastha.
- [Campbell, *Popular Tales of the West Highlands*, Volume IV](https://original.sacred-texts.com/neu/celt/pt4/pt417.htm): Fachan predates the game.
- [Yeats, *Irish Fairy and Folk Tales*](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d6/Irish_fairy_and_folk_tales_%28IA_yeatsirishfairy00yeatrich%29.pdf) and [Dúchas folklore collection](https://www.duchas.ie/en/cbes/4605938/4604080/4649856?Route=stories): Far Gorta traditions.
- [Museum Wales, Welsh Folklore](https://museum.wales/media/65056/Chwedlau-Cymru--Welsh-Folklore--A4-1.pdf): Gwraig Annwn.
- [Tolkien glossary](https://glossary.digitaltolkien.com/entry/ologhai.html): Olog-hai in *The Lord of the Rings* appendices.
- [Croker, *Fairy Legends and Traditions of the South of Ireland*](https://brittlebooks.library.illinois.edu/brittlebooks_open/Books2011-12/crokth0001faileg/crokth0001faileg.pdf): Dullahan.
- [Black Annis overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Annis): the English folklore association.
